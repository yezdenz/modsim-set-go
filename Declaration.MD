# Prepare the AI declaration accurately

Keep the declaration separate from the assessment when the user requests that arrangement. Do not add AI statements to the assessment body merely because a separate form is being prepared. Do not conceal a required disclosure; flag any conflict with the course's requirements.

Read the current form and assessment policy before filling it. Use the user's provided course, section, activity, name, date, learning code, and prompt parameters. Keep personal data out of public workflow examples.

## Classification and contribution

AI-Assisted and AI-Integrated describe the assessment's policy, not whether a small or large amount of AI was used. If the user and assessment provide different classifications, surface the mismatch and obtain the instructor-specific choice rather than silently selecting one.

Describe the actual contribution. If AI generated the model, code, calculations, explanations, checks, or document, say so. Do not describe substantial solution generation as grammar editing or imply that the student independently wrote or verified work that AI performed.

Complete a detailed section when the form requires it for expanded assistance. Short and detailed sections should agree about tools, purpose, material influence, verification, and the student's actual decisions.

## Prompts, references, and records

Use actual prompts or clearly labeled summaries. Do not turn the agent's own internal steps into invented user prompts. Do not claim the assessment's suggested example prompts were used unless they actually were.

List real verification sources and distinguish them from AI conversation records. A chat link is not an independent academic reference. Distinguish MATLAB execution, AI-run exact enumeration, user-run verification, and source inspection.

Describe images truthfully: rendered output captures and code images are not interface screenshots. Record attached conversation evidence only if it is actually attached. Do not generate a public share link or publish a private conversation merely to fill a blank without appropriate authorization.

Preserve the activity-related conversation when required. Do not fabricate or selectively reconstruct a transcript to make the record appear more favorable. Any necessary privacy redaction should be transparent and consistent with institutional rules.

## Review and completion

Fill factual fields and prepare responses for review. Leave unsupported personal attestations, signatures, and claims of understanding for the student. Do not tick a claim of having attached evidence when no attachment exists.

A request to fill a form does not automatically mean to submit it. Use the host's action rules for final submission or signing. If the form's output must be PDF, keep that requirement separate from a DOCX-only assessment preference and confirm the requested declaration deliverable when needed.

Verify that the completed fields persisted and the user can access the filled version. Do not say the declaration is submitted, signed, or complete when student-only fields, classification questions, or required records remain pending.
